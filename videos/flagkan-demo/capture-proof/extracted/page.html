The AI judge

The Jev judge (jev-latest) reads all six checks plus wallet-coordination signals and issues red / yellow / green with probabilities, a coordination score, and a severity rating. One failing check means red unless the judge finds strong counter-evidence. Green needs everything at once: whale under 15%, liquidity over $100k, age over 15 minutes.

Outcomes settle automatically: dead means the price fell 90%+ from first sight, liquidity drained under $1,000, or the price sat frozen with zero trading. Unresolved coins count down on the board; resolved coins stay 7 days. Holder data comes from Solami RPC — the only data path.
