## Brief (official description)

<p class="text-node"><strong>Meteora’s stack is built for builders wanting to innovate on asset creation.</strong><br><br>Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve (DBC) is a fully configurable token launch primitive: you control the curve shape, fee schedule, quote token, graduation threshold, and how the pool migrates into Meteora DAMM v2 liquidity.</p><h2 class="heading-node">Goals</h2><p class="text-node">We believe that there is a lot left to tokenize hence we need more innovative launchpads. <br><br>Meteora’s tech enables you build a launchpad that does stock-pairs, icm-pairs, meme-pairs, rwa-pairs and fully custom pair setups i.e. more asset classes on the same liquidity layer. <br><br>Our focus is on builders who want to experiment on ideas related to <strong>RWAs, Tokenized Stocks, AI, Memes </strong>etc. that's built on Meteora DBC and DAMM v2 tech.</p><h2 class="heading-node">Ideas we’d love to see</h2><ul class="list-node"><li><p class="text-node"><strong>Launch Mechanics tuned for Equity / Stocks paired launches</strong>: price discovery for thinly traded or newly tokenized names (xStocks, Backpack Onchain, Ondo RFQ, and the rest of the catalog).</p></li><li><p class="text-node"><strong>Novel Curve or Fee Configurations</strong> for a DBC token launch. For example. Flat Curve, Exponential Curve or Long Curve.</p></li><li><p class="text-node"><strong>Creative end-to-end launch flows</strong> using all of our stack (DBC, DAMM v2 and DLMM). For example, Conviction Pools with DLMM or Compounding Liquidity DAMM v2 Pools.</p></li><li><p class="text-node"><strong>Data Streams or Developer Tooling</strong> for trading terminals and builders to easily plug-and-play when building a launchpad.</p></li><li><p class="text-node"><strong>DBC Config Preset Marketplace</strong> - Popular launchpad configs that builders can easily pay-to-use.</p></li></ul><h2 class="heading-node">Judging Criteria</h2><ul class="list-node"><li><p class="text-node"><strong>Depth of Meteora integration</strong>: How central and meaningful is the use of the Meteora stack?</p></li><li><p class="text-node"><strong>Technical execution</strong>: Code quality, architecture decisions, and robustness of the implementation</p></li><li><p
